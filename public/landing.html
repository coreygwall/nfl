How it works

You run it, your friends play it. That's the whole thing.

Step 1

Pick a pool

Choose the game you want to run and the season it follows. Everything else is set up for you.

Step 2

One link to the group chat. Your friends type a name and they're playing — nothing to install.

Step 3

Watch the board

Scores land as games finish. Standings, bragging rights, and a running record of who called it.
